HUONG DAN CHAY LOCAL - QuanLyKhoLuuTru

1. Mo Windows PowerShell.

2. Chay lenh:

   cd D:\archive-management
   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\start-quanlykholuutru-local.ps1

3. Doi backend va frontend mo xong.

   Backend dung dia chi:
   http://127.0.0.1:8000

   Frontend dung dia chi:
   http://localhost:3000

4. Neu trinh duyet khong tu mo, mo Chrome/Edge va truy cap:

   http://localhost:3000

5. Dang nhap bang tai khoan admin:

   username: Admin
   password: Admin@123456789

6. Neu app khong mo duoc, kiem tra port bang lenh:

   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File D:\archive-management\scripts\check-ports.ps1

7. Neu port 8000 hoac 3000 dang bi chiem, dong cac cua so PowerShell backend/frontend cu roi chay lai script.

8. Khi su dung xong, co the dong cac cua so PowerShell backend va frontend.
